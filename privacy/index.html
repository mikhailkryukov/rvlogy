RVlogy privacy policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

RVlogy is an iPhone app made by Crystal RV Rentals, 1001 La Avenida St, Mountain View, CA ("we"). This policy explains what the app collects and why.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your location. The app uses it to show stops near you and, if you allow it, to notice when you arrive at a stop. Your position is never uploaded to us.
  • Your filters, your RV's height and length, and the list of people you blocked.

What you choose to share

When you post, these are saved in the app's public iCloud database (Apple CloudKit) so other RVers can see them:

  • Your thumbs up/down on a stop (one per person per stop), with the stop's location and roughly how far you were from it.
  • Reviews, ratings, price and detail updates, and new stops you add.
  • Photos you attach to a review. Photos are checked by us before other users can see them.
  • The display name you choose ("RVer" by default). If you tap "Continue with Apple", Apple shares your name with the app; we use only your first name and last initial as your display name. We don't request your email.
  • An anonymous iCloud user ID that Apple assigns to the app, so you can be blocked or moderated. We don't see your Apple ID or email.

Browsing works without posting anything. Posting requires being signed in to iCloud.

Reports and moderation

If you report a review or stop, we receive the report and the reason. We remove content that breaks the community rules and may block accounts that repeatedly do so.

What we don't do

No ads, no analytics or tracking SDKs, no selling or sharing of data with advertisers or data brokers.

Keeping and deleting your data

Public posts stay until they're removed. To delete your posts, contact us at info@crystalrv.com with your display name and the stops involved, and we'll delete them within 30 days. Deleting the app removes everything stored only on your iPhone.

Children

RVlogy isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes

We'll post any changes on this page and update the date above.
