RVlogy support

Questions, a stop that's wrong, or a post you want removed? Contact us at info@crystalrv.com and include the stop's name and city. We answer within 2 business days and act on reports of offensive content within 24 hours.

In the app

  • Report a review: press and hold it, then tap Report.
  • Block someone: press and hold their review, then tap Block. Undo in Settings → Blocked users.
  • Report a stop: open it, tap ⋯, then Report a problem (closed, duplicate, wrong location, not open to RVs).
  • Fix details or prices: open the stop and tap Update info.
  • Add a missing stop: tap ＋ or press and hold the map. New stops appear for everyone after a quick check.

Votes and your name

Each RVer has one 👍 or 👎 per stop. Tap the other button to change it. Your name on reviews is set in Settings: type it, or use Continue with Apple to fill in your first name and last initial.

Delete your posts

Contact us with your display name and the stops involved and we'll delete your reviews, photos and votes within 30 days.
